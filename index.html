Submission Guidelines

Format:

All submissions must be a single PDF file. We welcome high-quality original work in the following two tracks. References and appendices do not count toward the page limit, but the main text must be self-contained; reviewers are not required to read beyond it.

  • Full Papers: Up to 9 pages (excluding references) for novel methods, theoretical results, empirical studies, systems, benchmarks, or position papers on the path toward scientific super intelligence.
  • Tiny Papers: Up to 4 pages (excluding references) for early-stage ideas, simple implementations, modest theoretical results, follow-up experiments, negative results, or fresh perspectives on existing work.
ICLR Official

In line with the ICLR 2027 workshop guidelines, our Tiny Papers track prioritizes “less-than-full-conference” contributions and aims to broaden participation, especially from early-career researchers and authors outside the usual ML publication circuit. AI assistance is permitted, but Tiny Papers must be primarily human-authored; AI-generated papers are not allowed in this track. Authors of accepted papers may apply for ICLR 2027 Financial Assistance; see iclr.cc/Conferences/2027 for details.

Style file:

Please format your submission using the official ICLR 2027 LaTeX template. Include references and supplementary material in the same PDF. Submissions that alter the ICLR style (e.g., by decreasing margins or font sizes) or exceed the page limits may be rejected without review.

Dual-submission and non-archival policy:

We welcome ongoing and unpublished work. We will also consider papers that are under review elsewhere, provided this does not violate the dual-submission or anonymity policies of those venues. Since the workshop is meant to showcase new work, papers already published at a major ML venue (including the ICLR 2027 main conference) should not be submitted. The workshop is non-archival and will not have formal proceedings; accepted papers can be subsequently or concurrently submitted to other venues.

Visibility:

Submissions and reviews will not be public. Only accepted papers will be made public on OpenReview.

Double-blind reviewing:

All submissions must be anonymized and may not contain any identifying information that violates the double-blind reviewing policy. This applies to supplementary and linked material as well, including code; if you link to external material, you are responsible for ensuring anonymous browsing. Please do not include acknowledgements at submission time, and cite your own prior work in the third person. Papers that violate this policy will be desk-rejected.

Use of AI tools:

Authors and reviewers must follow the ICLR 2027 policies on AI use (authors, reviewers). Authors are fully responsible for the content of their submissions and must disclose their use of large language models as required by the ICLR 2027 policy.

Presentation:

ICLR 2027 workshops are held in person. All accepted papers will be presented as posters, and a selection will be invited for oral or spotlight talks. At least one author of each accepted paper is expected to attend; if no author can travel (e.g., due to visa issues), we will accommodate the paper with a pre-recorded video and a displayed poster.

Oral and Best Paper Awards:

We plan to select several papers for contributed oral talks and a set of Tiny Papers for short spotlight talks. We will present a Best Paper Award, a Best Tiny Paper Award, and a Best Poster Award decided by on-site voting.
